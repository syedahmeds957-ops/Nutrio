# Dish photo credits

One photo per kind of dish (see `src/tracker/dishImage.ts` for the keys:
drinks, desserts, rice, bbq, breakfast, stews, fast-food), saved here as
`<key>.jpg`, about 400 px wide. Only CC0, public-domain or CC-BY files.
After adding or removing one, run `node scripts/build-dish-images.mjs`.
